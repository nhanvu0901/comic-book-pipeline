# Specific micro-moment verification research

Verify ONE proposed single-issue moment. Do not enumerate alternatives and do
not improve the candidate — the only question is whether this exact moment, in
this exact issue, holds up.

CANDIDATE:
{candidate}

Find source text for this candidate's setup, specific action or reveal, and
direct consequence in the named issue. Return verbatim sentences and URLs;
add a second independent source when one exists. Separate what the source
states from what the scout inferred. A review's abstract phrase such as
"battle of truths" does not establish a physical action or a tactic.

State plainly:
- Is this from the COMIC, or from a film / TV / game adaptation?
- Does the moment actually occur in the named issue, or in a different one?
- Is the named subject the one the moment happens to?
- Is this series a REPRINT magazine? If so, name the original US issue.
- Does the issue number and year in the candidate match the sources?
- What exact action, decision, or admission changes the scene, and which source
  sentence supports it? Is the consequence in that same scene or narrow
  sequence?

Never treat publisher solicitation copy as evidence. A moment that is inferred,
belongs to another issue, or cannot be grounded in a retrieved source is
NOT CONFIRMED — say so rather than softening it.
