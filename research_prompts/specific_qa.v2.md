State plainly:
- Is this from the COMIC, or from a film / TV / game adaptation? If film/TV only,

issue number from a listicle or a multi-issue arc name. If you cannot find a
verbatim sentence describing the event in that specific issue, the verdict is
NOT CONFIRMED — do not soften it, and do not fill the gap from memory.
