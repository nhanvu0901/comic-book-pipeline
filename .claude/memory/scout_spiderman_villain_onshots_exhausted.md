# Spider-mythos villain/variant one-shots — confirmed exhausted (2026-06-30)

## Verdict
After full audit of Spider-Man-adjacent villain solos, symbiote non-events, and
deep-cut spider-variants on batcave.biz: **NO viable candidate exists** that passes
all pipeline gates (one-shot post-2010, ~22-45pp, acclaimed ≥8.0, no narration, no-brainer).

## What was checked and why rejected

### Villain solos
- Doctor Octopus, Lizard, Tombstone, Shocker, Sandman, Scorpion, Hammerhead — no one-shot post-2010 on batcave at all
- Kraven: only reprints of Kraven's Last Hunt (pre-2010 content)
- Morbius: Bond of Blood (2021) — 24pp, NO_NARRATION, but CBR rating 6.9/10 — too low ("forgettable", "clunky dialogue")

### Symbiote non-event one-shots
- Carnage: Black, White & Blood — 4-issue anthology (multiple creative teams per issue)
- Carnage Reigns: Alpha — 59pp (over limit) + event opener lore
- Carnage Reigns: Omega — 31pp but event Omega bookend, requires full event context
- Extreme Carnage: Toxin — 23pp but Extreme Carnage event tie-in (Knull codex)
- Absolute Carnage: Symbiote Spider-Man — Absolute Carnage event tie-in (Knull)
- King in Black: Spider-Man — HAS_NARRATION (Comicstorian Complete Story) + King in Black lore
- Venom: First Host — 5-issue mini, not one-shot
- Venom: Lethal Protector (2022) — 5-issue mini

### Deep-cut spider-variants
- Spider-Boy (2023): ongoing, no one-shot
- Spider-Punk: ongoing mini, no standalone one-shot
- Spider-Man India (2023): ongoing
- Silk: ongoing series only
- Spider-Woman (Jessica Drew): ongoing series only, no post-2010 one-shot on batcave
- Scarlet Spider / Ben Reilly: ongoing series, pre-2010 one-shots only

### Supporting cast
- No J. Jonah Jameson / Mary Jane / Aunt May / Black Cat standalone one-shots found post-2010

## Pattern
Spider-Man adjacent villain/variant content in 2010-2026 exists almost exclusively as:
1. Event tie-in one-shots (lore-heavy: Knull, Absolute Carnage, King in Black, Extreme Carnage)
2. Ongoing or mini-series (not one-shots)
3. Anthology books (multiple stories per issue)

**Do NOT hunt Spider-mythos villain/variant one-shots again — this vein is dry.**
Focus future scout runs on non-Spidey corners: X-Men, Iron Man, Thor, DC horror, Image.
